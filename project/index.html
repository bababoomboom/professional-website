WHAT IS WITHLOVE?

We know how much it can suck buying school supplies every year. Besides the school fee, there would be the due renewal of books and stationary. Wouldn't it be relieving to cut the costs and save up?

Of course we already have our system of asking a friend to ask a friend to ask a friend for borrowing, but it's pretty outdated. What if there was a more modern, connected and efficient way to ask around to donate?
